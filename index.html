Goal: Rapid technical assessment and recommendation during the tender phase to keep service costs to a minimum while providing an end-to-end perspective.

Scope:
  • Remote analysis of a defined technical or quality-related topic
  • Review of selected documents (e.g. specification, supplier documentation, inspection plan, etc.)
  • Short consultation including recommended actions

Goal: Structured assessment and a reliable basis for decision-making. Action plan and an overview of the required documents and deliverables.

Scope:
  • Technical / quality analysis of a project, supplier or work package
  • Identification of risks, deviations and optimization potential
  • Creation of a prioritized action plan, e.g. Inspection Test Plan, list of all documents, etc.
  • Final review meeting

Goal: Ongoing operational support in projects

Scope:
  • QA/QC support, engineering-related consulting or PMO-related tasks
  • Management of actions, reviews and coordination
  • Regular reporting and documentation
  • Flexible adaptation to project changes, scope and demand

Goal: Structured and complete review of technical documentation

Scope:
  • Review and compilation of supplier and project documentation
  • Definition of documentation structures
  • Support with turnover / final documentation
  • Coordination with customers, suppliers and the project team

Goal: Recommendations for modifications and upgrades to existing installations after completion

Scope:
  • Follow-up work after project completion
  • Cost optimization
  • Quality-related topics, e.g. NC, 8D, DFMEA, DRA, etc.
